# International SEO Localization Operations

## Market launch readiness
SEO launch approval depends on business readiness, not translation alone:
- target country and language validated;
- products/services available in that market;
- local pricing, currency, taxes, shipping, returns and payment options;
- native-language query research and competitor review;
- customer support and legal review;
- translated metadata, navigation, forms, trust content and transactional emails;
- locale URL and hreflang map;
- sitemap and canonical policy;
- analytics locale and currency configuration.

## Locale mapping
Maintain a mapping table with source locale, target locale, URL, language/region code, equivalence rationale, reciprocal links, canonical and launch status. Do not hreflang unrelated pages merely because they are translations in a broad sense.

## Translation QA
Review terminology, intent, units, decimal formats, dates, currency, cultural references, legal claims, product availability, keyword cannibalization, internal anchors and calls to action. Use native reviewers for high-value markets when possible.

## Launch tests
- each locale self-canonicalizes where intended;
- each alternate exists and returns the expected status;
- hreflang references are reciprocal and language/region codes valid;
- language switcher links to equivalent content, not always the homepage;
- fallback behavior is understandable and does not force redirect based solely on IP;
- locale sitemap includes only intended canonical URLs;
- Search Console/Bing properties and reporting are segmented by market where possible.

# SEO Measurement Plan

## Outcomes
Define the business result: qualified leads, purchases, signups, bookings, revenue, or another real outcome.

## Leading indicators
Search impressions/clicks, index coverage patterns, crawl errors, template performance, content discovery and query coverage.

## Segments
Market, locale, device, brand/non-brand, landing page, template, product category, and funnel stage.

## Baseline and evaluation
Record date range, seasonality, releases, tracking changes, attribution model, data limitations and comparison period. Avoid attributing causality from correlation alone.

## Experiment record
Hypothesis, affected cohort, implementation, guardrails, start/end date, success metric, failure criteria, observed result, confidence and decision.

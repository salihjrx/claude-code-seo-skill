# Global SEO Market Research Workbook

Create one row per market × locale × intent cluster. Do not combine markets just because they share a language.

## Required fields
`market | country | locale | script | language reviewer | search engine | query cluster | native query evidence | intent | SERP features | competitors | content format | product/service availability | price/currency | shipping/legal constraints | target URL | confidence | source | checked_at`

## Research sequence
1. Confirm audience and business availability in the market.
2. Gather native-language queries from first-party data and reliable tools.
3. Inspect current SERPs at documented locale/device/date.
4. Identify local competitors, marketplaces and local terminology.
5. Map one primary intent cluster to the most useful page type and URL.
6. Review translation/localization with a native speaker for critical pages.
7. Define market-specific measurement and conversion events.
8. Re-check after launch; market assumptions expire.

## Don't
- translate keyword lists literally;
- invent search volumes or CPCs;
- infer demand from one autocomplete result;
- assume a domain extension automatically ranks in a country;
- publish locale pages for markets the business cannot serve;
- treat language variants as interchangeable.

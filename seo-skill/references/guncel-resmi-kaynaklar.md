## Research policy
- SERP observations are date-, locale-, device- and query-specific.
- Ranking correlation does not prove causality.
- Tool metrics are not automatically search-engine metrics.
- A valid schema result does not guarantee rich results.
- Search visibility and AI citations cannot be guaranteed.

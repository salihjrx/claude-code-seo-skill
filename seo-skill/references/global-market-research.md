# Global Market Research Playbook

## Required output
Produce a market matrix and a query-to-URL map. Include research date, language/locale, device assumptions, source URLs and confidence. Separate observed SERP facts from hypotheses.

## Market matrix
For each target market record:
- locale and native language(s);
- actual search engines used by the audience (validate with data when possible);
- local query variants and terminology;
- local competitors and marketplaces;
- commercial availability, shipping, currency, support and policy differences;
- seasonal demand and local trust requirements;
- URLs/locales and conversion goal.

## SERP research
Sample representative head, mid-tail and long-tail queries. Record page types, SERP features, intent, recurring user needs, evidence quality and gaps. Do not infer universal ranking factors from small samples. Do not copy competitor content.

## Prioritization
Score opportunity qualitatively unless real metrics are available:
business value, evidence of demand, fit with offering, competitive feasibility, implementation effort, content cost and risk. Clearly identify assumptions.
